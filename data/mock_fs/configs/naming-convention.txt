NT IP-MPLS Device Naming Convention
====================================

Format:  <ROLE>-<SITE>-<NN>

ROLE
  CR   Core Router          backbone transit between sites
  PE   Provider Edge        terminates aggregation links and customer VRFs
  APE  Aggregation PE       collects traffic from local access routers
  LPE  Local PE             terminates customer access circuits

SITE (3-letter IATA-style code)
  BKK  Bangkok
  NBI  Nonthaburi

NN   two-digit sequence within the site

Examples
  CR-BKK-01    first core router in Bangkok
  APE-NBI-03   third aggregation PE in Nonthaburi
  LPE-NBI-11   local PE number 11 in Nonthaburi

Note: the sequence number is NOT contiguous. Numbers are reserved per rack row,
so gaps are normal and do not indicate a missing device.
